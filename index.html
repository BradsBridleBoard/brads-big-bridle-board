Model assumptions

A/B/C/D run left–right on this overhead plan at Y = +35.5/+24/−24/−35.5 respectively; beams 1/2 run vertically on the plan at X = −24/+24. Positions along A–D use X; along 1–2 use Y. The depicted scoreboard is 41′ × 41′, main bottom 95′, lower ring bottom 90′. Main top is drawn to 110′ visually only; collision calculations stop at the provisional 100′4″ top. Lower ring geometry is also provisional. Beam extents beyond known intersections are not verified.

Static tension is displayed only when the two 3D legs can balance a purely vertical load without an additional horizontal force. No dynamic, structural, connection, hardware, or out-of-plane restraint certification is implied. Use a qualified rigging professional to verify loads and assemblies.
